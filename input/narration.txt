Daily News for Saturday, November 14, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Anthropic's initial public offering is now reported to be several times oversubscribed, with bankers weighing pricing above the previously set 70 to 78 dollar per share range, a move that could push the company's valuation toward 1.5 trillion dollars ahead of trading expected around November 19.

SoftBank booked a 3.7 billion dollar charge in its latest quarterly results tied to advance purchases of power generation equipment for a U.S. AI data center project in Ohio, pushing the company to a 7 billion dollar quarterly net loss.

Quantum computing.

Atom Computing finalized a 100 million dollar research and development agreement with the U.S. Department of Commerce to scale up manufacturing of its fault tolerant, neutral atom quantum systems in Colorado.

Quantum software company Q Control raised a 290 million dollar funding round led by Salesforce Ventures, pushing its valuation above 1 billion dollars and funding expansion into finance, energy, and defense markets.

Researchers at the German Aerospace Center demonstrated a low cost error mitigation technique, validated on both IBM and IQM quantum processors, that improves measurement accuracy without extra computing overhead.

United States stock market.

Nvidia shares extended losses and dragged chip makers lower after chief executive Jensen Huang's resignation following reports of a second extramarital affair; HSBC downgraded the stock to Hold, and shares closed below a four trillion dollar market value with no successor yet named ahead of next week's earnings.

October's producer price index climbed six and a half percent from a year earlier, the fastest pace since 2022, pushing the 10 year Treasury yield to five point one seven percent and raising doubts the Federal Reserve will cut rates in December.

AI chip maker Cerebras Systems reported record quarterly revenue above 210 million dollars, with cloud revenue up 265 percent, and raised its full year outlook, sending shares up about 13 percent.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

Czech electricity and gas suppliers innogy and CEZ announced price increases for customers without fixed contracts starting in January, as wholesale gas prices have roughly doubled since spring due to the Middle East crisis.

The Czech government must decide within about a week whether to extend its fuel price cap and reduced diesel excise tax beyond November 30, a measure currently costing the budget over a billion crowns a month.

China.

Singapore barred former Megaspeed chief executive Alice Huang from re-entering the country after finding she misled police investigating a scheme to divert Nvidia chips to China, though insufficient evidence existed to charge her criminally.

Chinese taxpayers declared over 160 billion yuan in offshore trust income ahead of a new 20 percent tax deadline, helping drive October personal income tax revenue up nearly 90 percent year over year.
