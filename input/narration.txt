Daily News for Sunday, September 13, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

OpenAI's Sam Altman ruled out taking the company public in 2026, citing AI safety concerns as the reason a listing would be premature. The comments followed an essay from Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei urging AI labs to deliberately slow the pace of capability development, a call Altman said he agreed with.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

A European Commission audit found the Czech Republic may have to repay about six billion crowns in EU agricultural subsidies, after discovering the same land plots were used by multiple claimants. The Czech Agriculture Ministry disputes the finding and plans to contest it by the end of the year.

China.

Xi Jinping and other BRICS leaders adopted a declaration at their New Delhi summit committing to expand trade and payments in local currencies, launching a system linking India, China, and Russia's payment networks to reduce reliance on the dollar.
