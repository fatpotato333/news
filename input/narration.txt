Daily News for Tuesday, September 15, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Apple began rolling out Siri AI in beta, a rebuilt assistant with personal context awareness and cross app actions, launching in English first, with French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish following next month.

Anthropic launched Claude for Financial Advisors, an enterprise plugin connecting to wealth management platforms including BlackRock, Charles Schwab, and Addepar, with workflow tools for research, portfolio oversight, and administrative tasks.

Chinese AI lab Zhipu raised roughly five billion dollars, just two months after a four billion dollar round, earmarking the funds for its next generation GLM model, a new training framework, and expanded computing capacity.

ByteDance secured a syndicated loan of nearly thirty billion dollars to fund an AI infrastructure buildout that could see it spend as much as seventy billion dollars this year on data centers and computing power.

Quantum computing.

Nvidia expanded its CUDA-Q platform with a new tool called CUDA-Q Logical for simulating fault tolerant quantum systems before they are built; early users say it cut one algorithm's development time from five months to three weeks.

University of Pennsylvania physicists entangled four qubits in a room temperature diamond in under fifteen microseconds, about ten times faster than the standard method, pointing toward better quantum sensors and error correction.

Phasecraft and Nvidia reported a fifteen times speedup in quantum molecular simulations for drug discovery, generating thousands of simulation runs across thirteen molecules using Nvidia's Hopper GPUs.

United States stock market.

US stocks closed lower Monday as the AI slowdown debate hit chip stocks hard, with Nvidia, Intel, and AMD falling three to seven percent while Microsoft, Alphabet, and Meta rose; the ten year Treasury yield briefly topped five percent for the first time since 2007, as oil's climb stoked inflation concern ahead of this week's Federal Reserve meeting.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

Wholesale gas and electricity prices in Czechia and Slovakia jumped again this week, with gas up more than ten percent to about eighty euros per megawatt hour, the highest since 2022, as Middle East supply disruptions squeeze European energy markets and raise the odds the Czech government's contingency price cap gets triggered.

PPF Group.

E and PPF Telecom Group, the joint venture running PPF's telecom operations in Slovakia, Hungary, Bulgaria, and Serbia, reported first half revenue of one point three billion euros, up fourteen percent, with leverage improving to under its target level.

China.

New Chinese exit and entry regulations took effect today, giving authorities expanded power to scrutinize visa and entry applications, impose multi year entry bans for false information, and newly regulate immigration consulting firms.

China's August data showed retail sales grew just zero point four percent year on year, the weakest pace since May, while industrial output beat forecasts at five point two percent, adding pressure on Beijing for further stimulus.
