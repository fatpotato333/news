Daily News for Wednesday, September 16, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Independent analysis firm SemiAnalysis published pre-release benchmark testing showing NVIDIA's unreleased Rubin NVL72 platform reaching about fifty nine point four million tokens per second per megawatt, versus twenty eight point five million for the current GB300 Blackwell platform, at matched interactivity, with the efficiency gap widening further at higher throughput.

Cornelis Networks, an Intel networking spinoff, raised two hundred five million dollars led by IAG Capital Partners and unveiled Active Compute Fabric, a GPU agnostic in-network computing architecture built on open Ethernet and Ultra Ethernet standards, meant to challenge NVIDIA's interconnect dominance in AI data centers, alongside a new rack scale networking collaboration with Qualcomm.

Perplexity and NVIDIA released Portable Computer for Windows, a fully on-device agentic AI assistant for RTX graphics cards with at least twenty four gigabytes of memory, running its model, orchestrator and task scheduler locally with no cloud connection required.

An investigation revealed OpenAI runs an internal program called Project Lily, in which hundreds of contractors read real ChatGPT conversations, with usernames and personal details supposedly stripped, to rate and improve model responses, though sensitive details can still leak through. OpenAI confirmed the practice and said Anthropic runs a similar review program of its own.

Quantum computing.

USC and Quantum Elements published peer reviewed research in Nature Communications showing a surface code error correction scheme, combined with dynamical decoupling to suppress idle qubit errors, achieved below threshold logical qubit performance on IBM's real Heron quantum processors, despite a mismatch between the code's grid layout and the chip's native connectivity.

Quantum hardware design firm QC Design integrated its Plaquette platform with NVIDIA's newly launched CUDA-Q Logical simulation layer and found that a leakage rate of just zero point two percent on two qubit gates cuts a fault tolerant circuit's noise threshold by roughly sixty percent, a concrete warning sign for how leakage erodes real hardware error correction margins.

United States stock market.

US stocks fell for a second straight session Tuesday, with the S and P 500 down zero point four five percent to seven thousand five hundred eighty five, the Nasdaq down zero point seven eight percent to about twenty five thousand nine hundred eighty two, and the Dow down three hundred twenty eight points to fifty two thousand ninety three, as investors braced for the Federal Reserve's rate decision and Treasury yields stayed near multi decade highs.

Bank of America shares fell as much as five point seven percent after CEO Brian Moynihan warned that third quarter trading revenue would be roughly flat year over year, a reversal from the surge seen in the first half, and that investment banking fees could decline more than ten percent, a warning that also weighed on Asian bank stocks the following session.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

The Czech governing coalition failed to agree on reinstating a three day unpaid waiting period at the start of sick leave, after part of ANO and SPD sided with unions and the opposition against it. Minister Boris Šťastný confirmed the plan has fallen through for now; it would have saved the state budget an estimated five billion crowns a year, at the direct expense of workers' sick pay.

Slovak lawmakers gave final approval to a building act amendment that eases paperwork for minor construction, adjusts building authority procedures, and allows retroactive legalization of some unauthorized construction. It affects developers, contractors and property owners, with most provisions taking effect January first, twenty twenty seven.

Slovak parliament also passed a reform letting a new state run entity perform vehicle technical and emissions inspections alongside private operators, while extending inspection validity from two years to three and easing staffing requirements, effective January first, twenty twenty seven.
