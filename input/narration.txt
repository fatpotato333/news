Daily News for Sunday, August 2, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

OpenAI unveiled a new model family called Astra, saying an internal version produced machine-checked proofs for ten previously unsolved problems in mathematics and theoretical computer science, some open for over a decade, at an estimated compute cost near two thousand dollars, and opened free access to the tool for one hundred thousand academic researchers.

China.

China's Southern Theater Command carried out joint naval and air combat drills near Scarborough Shoal on August first, testing reconnaissance, deterrent patrols and joint strike capabilities with supersonic anti-ship missiles, after Beijing objected to a Philippine move to expand seabed claims off Palawan toward the reef.
