Daily News for Thursday, November 19, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Anthropic priced its initial public offering at 83 dollars per share, above the raised range it had set, selling about 81 billion dollars worth of stock in the largest public offering on record. The deal values the company near 1.5 trillion dollars, with trading on the Nasdaq under the ticker A N T set to begin today.

United States stock market.

Major indexes fell again on Tuesday despite Nvidia's strong earnings report. The Dow dropped 561 points to its lowest close since June, and the S and P 500 slipped for a fifth straight session, even as the Nasdaq edged higher. Nvidia shares fell nearly 3 percent after company leadership ruled out bringing back former CEO Jensen Huang, overshadowing revenue that roughly doubled from a year earlier.

Health insurers UnitedHealth and Humana fell sharply after regulators proposed a rule to claw back Medicare Advantage overpayments dating back to 2018. The Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services estimates the rule could recover about 9 billion dollars a year industry wide. A public comment period runs into January.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

The Czech Banking Association cut its 2026 growth forecast to 1.7 percent and now expects interest rates to stay near 4 percent through all of next year, citing a worsening inflation outlook.

Czech household debt jumped 16 percent this year to over 4.5 trillion crowns, driven largely by homeowners refixing old low-rate mortgages at today's higher rates, which pushes up loan balances and monthly payments.

A new European survey found 41 percent of Czechs who don't own a home now believe they never will, compared with a 27 percent average across the European Union, with most blaming low income relative to housing costs.

China.

Donald Trump and Xi Jinping held a bilateral meeting in Shenzhen on the sidelines of the APEC summit, followed by an unprecedented three way meeting with Vladimir Putin. No joint trade statement was issued. Xi pledged deeper cooperation on artificial intelligence and trade, while Trump said Washington is negotiating several new trade agreements with Pacific nations.
