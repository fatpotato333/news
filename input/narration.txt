Daily News for Monday, November 2, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Google completed its first artificial intelligence inference from orbit, running its Gemma four model on a tensor processing unit aboard the prototype Suncatcher satellite and sending back a short AI generated poem. The test, flown with partner Planet since an October first launch, is an early step toward space based AI data centers, not yet a working system.

United States stock market.

Blackstone now expects Anthropic may receive only a speculative grade credit rating after its initial public offering, reversing earlier expectations of an investment grade score, the Financial Times reported. Blackstone holds about nine billion dollars of junior debt in Anthropic's financing structure, raising questions about the terms behind the AI buildout.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

The Czech government sent parliament a bill imposing a fifty percent special tax on oil refinery profits, aimed largely at Orlen Unipetrol's two domestic refineries. The measure is expected to raise about five and a half billion crowns a year to help close the twenty twenty seven state budget deficit and has not yet passed.
