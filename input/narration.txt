Artificial intelligence.

OpenAI is cutting off the coding tool Cursor's access to its AI models this November, after Elon Musk's SpaceX bought Cursor's maker, Anysphere, for about sixty billion dollars, saying it cannot trust a Musk controlled company to honor its usage terms.

Anthropic warned that malware already sitting on some users' computers has been hijacking their logged in Claude sessions to quietly burn through usage limits, and the company is now force logging out affected accounts and refunding unauthorized charges.

Chinese AI lab DeepSeek is closing in on a funding round of about seven point four billion dollars at a valuation near seventy four billion dollars, money earmarked for roughly a gigawatt of new computing capacity as it eyes a future Shanghai stock listing.

United States forces struck Iranian rocket launchers near the Strait of Hormuz on Sunday, after Revolutionary Guard forces were seen preparing to fire mine laden rockets into the waterway. Czech Republic and Slovakia.

The Czech government reviewed a labor code amendment that would bar employers from asking job candidates about their previous salary, requiring them instead to disclose minimum pay and benefits before signing a contract, transposing a new European Union pay transparency law.

Czech ministers also reviewed a new platform work law extending employee style protections to gig workers on delivery and ride hailing apps. More than half a million Czechs currently work through such platforms, and the law is set to take effect from January twenty twenty seven.

China.

China's official manufacturing index rose slightly to forty nine point eight in August, beating forecasts but marking a second straight month below the fifty point growth threshold, as the world's second largest economy continues to struggle with weak domestic demand.

China updated the toll from last week's glacier collapse flood on the Nepal border to sixteen dead, with two hundred sixty one foreign nationals from twenty three countries still missing. Authorities have mobilized about two thousand one hundred rescue workers and allocated more than thirty two million dollars in relief funds while monitoring an unstable debris lake upstream.
