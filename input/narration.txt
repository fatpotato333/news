Daily News for Saturday, October 10, 2026.

Quantum computing.

IonQ reported entangling a trapped ion qubit with a solid state quantum memory over a photonic link at more than 1,000 entanglement events per second, over four times the previous trapped ion record set by a collaborating Duke University team. The underlying numbers come from a company release and an unreviewed preprint tied to a DARPA research program.

United States stock market.

Wall Street closed higher Friday, with the Dow up 0.83%, the S and P 500 up 0.59%, and the Nasdaq up 0.64%, posting weekly gains as AI related stocks rebounded on a Bloomberg report that OpenAI's annualized revenue could reach 70 billion dollars by year end, partly reversing Thursday's selloff triggered by a lower Financial Times estimate.

Delta Air Lines cut its full year earnings guidance to between 5 dollars 10 cents and 5 dollars 60 cents per share, down from 6 50 to 7 50, after third quarter profit missed expectations on roughly 6 billion dollars in added fuel costs.

The University of Michigan's preliminary October consumer sentiment index fell to 46.3 from September's 48.1, while year ahead inflation expectations rose to 4.7%.

China.

China's Ministry of Finance released 550 billion yuan, about 82 billion dollars, in unused local government debt quotas, with 300 billion earmarked for county and district government operating costs and the rest for infrastructure, as Beijing follows through on its fiscal support pledges amid soft economic data.
