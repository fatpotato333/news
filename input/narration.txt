Daily News for Sunday, November 15, 2026.

China.

China's central bank raised the reserve requirement ratio on foreign currency deposits from four percent to six percent, effective today, draining an estimated twenty four billion dollars of dollar liquidity from the banking system in an effort to slow the yuan's rally. The onshore yuan weakened to its lowest level in about two weeks, near six point six eight per dollar, after the central bank set a softer than expected daily currency fixing.
