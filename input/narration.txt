Daily News for Sunday, August 9, 2026.

China.

China ordered ships in the southern Taiwan Strait to follow storm related traffic control instructions as Typhoon Dolphin made landfall on the Zhejiang and Fujian coast. Taiwan's government called the directive utterly ridiculous, saying Beijing has no authority over international waters, and told vessels to disregard it.

China's July consumer prices rose zero point five percent from a year earlier but slipped zero point one percent from June, while core inflation rose zero point nine percent. Factory gate prices rose three point five percent year over year.
