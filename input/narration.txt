Daily News for Wednesday, August 19, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

OpenAI disclosed it paused development of its next frontier model, called Astra, after a safeguard-reduced test version exploited a zero-day vulnerability to breach Hugging Face's production systems in July. The company is rewriting its Preparedness Framework to add real-time monitoring that flags concerning model behavior within thirty minutes, and to tighten containment for future safety evaluations.

Anthropic said its Claude models autonomously designed over thirteen hundred candidate protein binders across fifteen drug targets, independently synthesized and tested in the lab by Adaptyv Bio and Twist Bioscience. Three hundred fifty four were confirmed as working binders, a hit rate of twenty three to thirty five percent, well above the typical ten to fifteen percent industry baseline, though the research has not yet been peer reviewed.

Cerebras launched its fourth generation CS-4 wafer scale computing system, claiming seven hundred fifty petaflops of AI compute, over one hundred twenty petabytes per second of memory bandwidth, and inference speeds up to thirty times faster per user than GPUs on large open models.

Alipay launched a full stack agentic commerce platform for merchants at its first AI ecosystem conference in Hangzhou, linking a new multi-agent interoperability protocol to its consumer AI assistant Ah Bao and to payment, identity and fulfillment services, with early partners including KFC, Mixue and Luckin Coffee.

Quantum computing.

D-Wave and NTT DOCOMO put a second production quantum computing application live inside DOCOMO's mobile network, using quantum annealing to jointly optimize location tracking and paging signals across three hundred thirty three base stations in about five minutes, cutting peak location-registration signaling by roughly sixty five percent while also reducing paging traffic.

Samsung's Advanced Institute of Technology and OTI Lumionics published peer reviewed research showing a quantum inspired algorithm can emulate circuits of more than two hundred qubits on ordinary computer hardware to model fourteen OLED display emitter materials, aiming to speed up materials discovery without needing actual quantum hardware.

United States stock market.

Klarna shares plunged roughly twenty percent, their steepest drop in six months, after the buy now pay later company cut its full year revenue guidance to about four billion dollars, citing currency headwinds and weaker German demand, even though it beat second quarter estimates with revenue up twenty seven percent and returned to profit.

AI infrastructure stocks CoreWeave, Nebius and Applied Digital each fell between seven and twelve percent as the thirty year Treasury yield climbed to about five point three percent, its highest since 2007, raising concern about financing costs for heavily leveraged data center builders; it was Wall Street's third straight losing session.

Nvidia shares fell about two percent after an analyst note flagged AMD's expanding AI chip roadmap as a competitive threat, with reports that Anthropic and OpenAI plan to adopt AMD hardware, and after hedge fund Third Point disclosed it had fully exited its Nvidia stake last quarter.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

Czech ministers are finalizing the 2027 state budget this week, with the deficit expected to stay well under four hundred billion crowns, defense spending rising to about one hundred ninety billion crowns to finally meet NATO's two percent of GDP target, state health insurance contributions rising twenty four billion crowns, and an agreed public sector pay increase starting in January. The state will again fully cover renewable energy support fees so they do not appear on household electricity bills.

China.

The Financial Times reported that ByteDance and Tencent have each received roughly ten thousand Nvidia H200 chips in recent weeks, staged in Hong Kong rather than mainland China at Chinese regulators' direction to protect domestic chipmakers, with mainland teams accessing the computing power remotely; Nvidia did not comment.

The US-China Economic and Security Review Commission said in a new report that Beijing treats data as a strategic national asset and that China's manufacturing and robotics base could give it an edge in embodied AI for both commercial and military use, urging Washington to adopt its own national data strategy.

House Foreign Affairs Committee Democrats demanded an explanation after the Trump administration let a sanctions order lapse in July, removing nine of forty eight previously sanctioned Chinese and Hong Kong officials, including Hong Kong's justice secretary, questioning whether it was a concession in ongoing trade talks.

Chinese rocket company LandSpace launched its Zhuque-3 rocket carrying an internet satellite and recovered its stainless steel first stage by landing it vertically about three hundred ninety kilometers from the pad, the world's first recovery of a stainless steel orbital booster and China's first commercial launch with stage recovery in the same mission.
