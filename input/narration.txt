Daily News for Monday, August 10, 2026.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

The average Czech mortgage rate climbed to five point four two percent in early August, the fifth straight monthly increase and the highest level in two years, according to Hypoindex data. Monthly payments on a typical mortgage are now roughly eleven hundred korunas higher than in March, and analysts expect rates to plateau rather than fall soon.

China.

Flooding from Typhoon Dolphin forced more than one million evacuations in Zhejiang province, swamped over one hundred roads in Shanghai, and canceled about fifteen hundred flights, with a power plant in Zhejiang also knocked out as heavy rain continued into Monday.

Chinese AI chipmaker Moore Threads won board approval for a Hong Kong stock listing, following a nearly four hundred twenty percent share surge since its Shanghai debut in December. First half revenue rose one hundred forty seven percent year over year, though the company remains unprofitable.

Humanoid robot maker Unitree opened subscription for its Shanghai stock market listing, pricing shares to raise about six point one billion yuan at a valuation near sixty one billion yuan, well above the target it set five days earlier, making it the first profitable humanoid robotics company to go public in China.
