Daily News for Monday, August 24, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

At the World Humanoid Robot Games in Beijing, a robot called Tien Kung Omni from Chinese robotics firm X-Humanoid won its 400 meter race with an unusual forward-leaning stride and arms held near its face. Engineers said reinforcement learning produced this gait because it reduces shoulder strain and preserves leg power better than a human-style arm swing.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

The Czech government is set to discuss a pension savings overhaul today that would double state contributions to 40 percent of deposits for savers under 29, let savers under 36 withdraw a third of their funds early without penalty, cap annual management fees at half a percent, and wind down older transformed pension funds by the end of 2036, at an estimated cost of 1.1 to 1.6 billion crowns a year.

Later today, the government, trade unions, and employers hold extraordinary talks on raising the minimum wage from 22,400 crowns to 24,900 crowns in 2027, and to 26,900 crowns in 2028, moving it toward 47 percent of the average wage by 2029. The same session opens separate talks on 2027 public sector pay, projected to rise about 6 percent.

China.

Beijing has activated a level four emergency response across seven provinces, including Hainan, Guangdong, and Zhejiang, as three typhoons remain active in the region at once, a rare occurrence in a year that has already seen 20 typhoons versus a historical average of about 13. Typhoon Saudel is forecast to strengthen into a super typhoon near Japan, while typhoon Narra is tracking toward landfall near the Guangxi-Vietnam border around August 26.
