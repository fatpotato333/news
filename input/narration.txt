Daily News for Sunday, September 27, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Google has begun testing a feature in India that lets users buy select Flipkart products, including phones and electronics, directly inside Gemini and AI Mode, using an in-chat checkout button. A wider rollout is planned for October ahead of India's festive shopping season.

United States stock market.

The University of Michigan's final September consumer sentiment index fell to 48.1, a four month low and the second straight monthly decline. Year ahead inflation expectations jumped to 4.6 percent, the highest since June, with analysts citing elevated fuel prices and renewed trade tensions.

China.

China's foreign ministry said the United States and Chinese militaries have agreed in principle to a new crisis communication pact meant to prevent dangerous encounters in the Indo-Pacific, following last week's Xi-Trump summit. Washington has not yet publicly confirmed the agreement.

Chinese officials also detailed further outcomes from that summit: reciprocal tariff cuts on about 30 billion dollars of goods, a Chinese pledge to buy at least 10 million metric tons of American coal in 2027 and 2028, and a new bilateral channel specifically for discussing incidents involving advanced AI systems, which officials referred to as superintelligence.
