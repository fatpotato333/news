Daily News for Thursday, October 29, 2026.

Quantum computing.

University of Sydney researchers working with IBM linked three logical qubits on IBM's new Nighthawk processor, preparing entangled logical states and holding error suppression steady across 170 rounds of error correction. The result is a preprint, not yet peer reviewed, but a genuine hardware demonstration.

A team including NVision Imaging Technologies showed in a peer reviewed Nature paper that a single engineered molecule can serve as an individually controllable quantum bit using light, with millisecond-scale memory at just above four Kelvin. NVision is now partnering with HyperLight to put the molecules onto photonic chips.

Utility company EPB in Chattanooga and IonQ used a trapped-ion quantum computer to help optimize power grid routing, cutting a classical solver's runtime on a large network from about 24 minutes to under 35 seconds in early testing, under a National Institute of Standards and Technology grant.

United States stock market.

The Federal Reserve held interest rates steady at 3.75 to 4 percent in a 10 to 2 vote, with two officials pushing for a hike instead. The 10-year Treasury yield rose above 5 percent, and markets now expect a possible rate cut in December.

Microsoft beat earnings expectations for its first fiscal quarter, with revenue up 19 percent and Azure cloud growth of 48 percent, though free cash flow fell as capital spending nearly doubled. Shares rose about 3 percent after hours.

Alphabet's quarterly revenue rose 25 percent and Google Cloud revenue more than doubled on surging demand for its AI chips, but profit was dented by an investment loss and capital spending nearly doubled, pushing free cash flow negative. Shares fell about 10 percent.

Oracle shares jumped about 12 percent after the company set a target of more than 225 billion dollars in annual revenue by 2030 and capped new borrowing, easing investor worries about the cost of its AI data center buildout.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

A Czech National Bank board member signaled the bank will likely raise its key rate to 4 percent on November 5th to fight energy-driven inflation. Average mortgage rates have already climbed to 5.79 percent, adding thousands of crowns a year to typical payments.

European wholesale gas prices briefly topped 100 euros per megawatt hour, the highest since 2022, after Gulf tensions curbed Qatari exports. Slovakia's regulator says fixed 2027 household prices won't change, but industrial users on market contracts face immediate higher costs.

Slovak President Peter Pellegrini signed the 2027 state budget into law, locking in a deficit of nearly 4.9 percent of gross domestic product. The finance ministry says further austerity measures will be needed to bring it down in later years.

Pellegrini also signed a law guaranteeing Slovaks aged 85 and older with at least 30 years of pension contributions a minimum pension of 710 euros a month starting in January, with back pay for those who qualify.

China.

A European Parliament trade delegation met China's commerce minister in Beijing to press concerns over the European Union's roughly one billion euro a day trade deficit with China, market access, and rare earth supplies. No new agreement was reached, with further talks set for early next year.
