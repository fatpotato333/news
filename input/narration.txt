Artificial intelligence.

A federal appeals court upheld the Pentagon's decision to blacklist Anthropic as a national security supply chain risk, after Anthropic refused to let the military use Claude for, quote, all lawful purposes, citing limits on autonomous weapons and domestic surveillance. The ruling was stayed pending a possible appeal.

Microsoft shares rose three to four percent on the announcement.
