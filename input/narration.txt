Daily News for Sunday, November 22, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Mathematicians in the Lean and Mathlib community say they now have full confidence in the correctness of a formally verified proof of the Riemann Hypothesis, developed by Anthropic researcher Levent Alpöge working with the Claude AI model, after independently re-checking its roughly seventeen million lines of Lean code using two separate verification kernels. Experts caution that confirming the formal statement truly matches the Riemann Hypothesis, and producing a human-readable version other mathematicians can check line by line, is still pending and may take another week or two.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

The Czech Supreme Audit Office reported that the state budget ran a first-half deficit of almost 184 billion koruna, the worst first-half result since 2023, as mandatory spending rose faster than revenue and state debt climbed to about 3.7 trillion koruna. The Finance Ministry says the shortfall is seasonal and should narrow once second-half revenue catches up.

A new ČSOB survey found Slovak families sharply changing habits to cope with rising living costs. Ninety one percent now shop mainly by promotions and discounts, up to 88 percent cook at home or bring lunch to work rather than eating out, and most have cut energy use, while some have paused savings, insurance payments, or turned to overdrafts and credit cards to get by.
