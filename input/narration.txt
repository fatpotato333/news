Artificial intelligence.

The Federal Trade Commission sent formal civil investigative demands to OpenAI and Anthropic, seeking records on AI safety incidents, security safeguards and marketing claims, and plans to question senior executives under oath; the agency says the demands are not themselves a finding of wrongdoing.

Microsoft AI announced a new foundation model called MAI-Base-2, trained on sixteen thousand Nvidia GB200 chips over thirty eight days, which it says pretrains several times more efficiently than a comparable DeepSeek model; Microsoft plans to license it to partners through its Foundry platform.

Meta released an open-weight model called Muse Spark Open, an eight hundred sixteen billion parameter multimodal system built for coding and long agentic tasks, which Meta says can run locally on two Apple Mac Studio computers, a first for a model of its scale on desktop hardware.

Consulting firm Accenture capped employee spending on Anthropic's Claude at twenty nine dollars a month after internal token costs surged, with some staff previously spending up to a thousand dollars monthly, according to an internal memo reported by 404 Media.

Quantum computing.

IonQ opened its first university-based research center, at the University of Cambridge, installing a two hundred fifty six qubit quantum computer and a campus quantum network to support nine joint projects spanning optical interconnects, portfolio optimization, structural engineering and cancer target identification.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

At an EU summit in Brussels, Czechia led Slovakia, Poland and Hungary in pushing to delay a new carbon price on transport fuel and home heating until twenty thirty, which would postpone added costs at the pump and on heating bills for households; no agreement was reached on the first day.

The Czech Chamber of Deputies overrode a presidential veto to pass a new civil service law that makes it easier to dismiss state employees during restructuring and replaces a paid redeployment pool for displaced officials with direct severance pay.

Slovakia's parliament approved the twenty twenty seven state budget, with a deficit of close to five percent of GDP and no new taxes, including an eight point four percent public sector pay rise and a pension increase of roughly three and a half percent.

China.

Wealthy mainland Chinese individuals are rushing to unwind offshore trusts and raise cash ahead of an October twenty second deadline under a new twenty percent tax on offshore trust income, with tax officials using rich list data to identify targets and some facing bills in the hundreds of millions of yuan.
