DeepSeek's new peak and off-peak pricing for its API took effect, with peak-hour output costs for some models rising by as much as eleven hundred percent as the company reallocates capacity to manage surging demand.

China.

China postponed release of its July economic data, covering industrial output, retail sales, investment, and property prices, from ten in the morning to three in the afternoon Beijing time, so it would not overlap with President Xi Jinping's speech marking the hundredth anniversary of former leader Jiang Zemin's birth. Economists expect the delayed figures to show continued weakness in credit growth.
