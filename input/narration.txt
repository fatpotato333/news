Artificial intelligence.

XAI, now folded into SpaceX, released Grok four point seven, a two point one trillion parameter flagship model priced at two dollars per million input tokens, which the company says leads on cost efficiency and new biosecurity and cybersecurity safety benchmarks.

United States stock market.

The Nasdaq closed at a record high, up two point three percent, and the S and P 500 had its best day since early August, as AI and chip stocks rallied on falling oil prices and optimism ahead of the Trump-Xi summit; AMD's market value topped one trillion dollars for the first time, and Intel and Arm also jumped sharply.

Bitcoin rose above eighty seven thousand dollars, its highest level since January, lifting shares of Coinbase and Robinhood by more than five percent each as falling oil prices boosted risk appetite broadly.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

The Czech government approved its twenty twenty seven budget, with a record three hundred eighty six billion koruna deficit, up from three hundred ten billion this year, public sector wage increases of five to nine percent, healthcare funding up more than fifty billion koruna, and defense spending reaching two percent of GDP for the first time.

The government also confirmed pensions will rise again in January, lifting the average old age pension by over three hundred koruna a month to about twenty two thousand koruna, plus a one time two thousand koruna payment to every pensioner, covering roughly two point three million retirees at a total cost of ten point three billion koruna.

Slovakia's first government bond auction since summer saw yields jump to as high as four and a half percent, well above a month earlier, signaling higher government borrowing costs ahead.
