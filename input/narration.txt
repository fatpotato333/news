Daily News for Monday, September 21, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

SoftBank is seeking more than eleven billion dollars in junk bonds, one of the largest such deals on record, to fund the third payment of its investment in OpenAI, bringing its total commitment there to nearly sixty five billion dollars.

Chinese startup StepFun launched Step Five Preview, a six hundred billion parameter agentic model priced at roughly one seventh the cost of leading rivals, matching Kimi K three Max on independent benchmarks, with open weights promised next month.

Alibaba's Qwen team open sourced Qwen Image two point one, a seven billion parameter model that unifies image generation and editing, supporting transparent backgrounds and multiple reference images, released under a noncommercial research license.

China.

The United States and China agreed to launch a formal bilateral dialogue on artificial intelligence, including a mechanism for each side to alert the other to AI related security incidents, following weekend talks in New York ahead of this week's summit between President Trump and President Xi in Washington.

A fire at a textile factory in Foshan, in southern China's Guangdong province, killed at least eight people on Sunday, with the cause still undisclosed and search and rescue operations continuing.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

The Czech government is preparing to cut fuel excise duty by about four crowns per liter and introduce price caps at the pump, after diesel prices climbed to their highest level since 2022.

The Czech National Budget Council warned that meaningful reduction of the state budget deficit in 2028 and 2029 is unlikely, contradicting the government's own consolidation plan, as debt servicing costs keep rising.

Czech resale apartment prices hit a historic high, with Prague averaging over one hundred fifty two thousand crowns per square meter, up roughly thirteen thousand crowns from a year earlier.
