Daily News for Thursday, August 27, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Amazon Web Services and Nvidia agreed to deploy two million additional Nvidia GPUs, including Blackwell Ultra and Rubin chips, across AWS infrastructure through 2027 and 2028, on top of an earlier one million GPU commitment, and will build a dedicated 100,000 GPU AI factory for United States government workloads.

OpenAI unveiled Jalapeño, its first custom inference chip, built with Broadcom and Celestica, claiming close to double the throughput per watt of Nvidia's Blackwell chips in its own benchmarks; limited deployment begins this year with volume production in 2027.

Alibaba released Qwen 3.8 Flash Next, a 125 billion parameter model previewing its next generation Qwen 4 architecture, which the company says matches its larger Qwen 3.7 Plus model at about one ninth the training cost.

Moonshot AI is in early talks with Microsoft, Amazon, and Google to host its Kimi K3 model on their cloud platforms in exchange for about 30 percent of related service revenue, which would be the first such deal between a Chinese AI firm and a major American cloud provider.

United States stock market.

Nvidia reported fiscal second quarter revenue of 96.2 billion dollars, up 106 percent from a year earlier, with earnings per share of 2.22 dollars, and guided next quarter's revenue to 108 billion dollars, above analyst estimates. The company also announced a 25 billion dollar share buyback, and shares rose about 4.7 percent in after hours trading.

Meta agreed to pay up to 17 billion dollars over 10 years to settle child safety lawsuits brought by 47 states, one of the largest state consumer protection settlements on record, requiring default two hour daily time limits and a midnight to 6 a.m. usage block for users under 18.

The S&P 500 rose 0.3 percent and the Nasdaq gained 0.7 percent, a third straight day of gains, after July's personal consumption expenditures inflation index held at 3.7 percent year over year, reinforcing expectations the Federal Reserve holds interest rates steady in September.

Abercrombie and Fitch shares jumped about 36 percent after earnings beat estimates, boosted by roughly 100 million dollars in tariff refunds, and the company raised its full year sales and profit margin guidance.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

Slovakia's 2027 minimum wage will rise automatically to 972 euros, up 6.2 percent from 915 euros, after employers and unions failed to reach agreement by the legal deadline, affecting about 182,000 workers.

Slovakia's government approved a bill banning children under 16 from creating or using social media accounts, part of a broader anti-radicalization law set to take effect in 2027.

China.

Typhoon Saudel, the season's eighteenth named storm, is approaching landfall on China's southeastern coast between Zhejiang and Fujian, prompting evacuation of more than 4,100 tourists, suspension of dozens of ferry routes, and forecasts of up to 500 millimeters of rain.
