Daily News for Saturday, August 15, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Apple has built its own proprietary AI model for the Chinese market with technical assistance from Alibaba, reportedly becoming the first foreign company cleared by Beijing to offer a self developed AI model there. Apple had previously planned to rely on Alibaba's own Qwen model, and the new rollout is expected within the coming months.
Zhipu AI released its GLM 5.3 model, claiming a fifty percent jump in coding capability over the prior version from post training improvements alone, and the top rank among open weight models on two major coding and agent benchmarks. The company also reported the model found over two thousand four hundred security vulnerabilities across more than two hundred software projects during testing, a success rate above eighty percent on a leading cybersecurity benchmark, with open weights following in about two weeks after a security review.

Quantum computing.

Quantinuum and Taiwan's Quanta Computer announced a partnership to jointly engineer modular, manufacturable hardware for large scale, fault tolerant quantum computers, aiming to industrialize quantum systems production, with joint engineering work already underway; no investment figure was disclosed.

United States stock market.

Broadcom shares fell about six percent after Bank of America questioned the scale of the company's AI chip financing arrangements, estimating its related debt could reach roughly three hundred seventy billion dollars by 2029, fueling broader concern about how AI infrastructure spending is being funded, even as Broadcom's own guidance still points to AI semiconductor revenue roughly tripling from a year earlier.
Reddit shares jumped about thirteen percent after S&P Dow Jones Indices said Reddit will join the S&P 500, making it only the second pure play social media company in the index after Meta, with index funds needing to buy shares ahead of the effective date next week.
Nu Holdings, parent of Nubank, rose about ten percent after posting its first ever quarter above one billion dollars in net profit, up forty nine percent from a year earlier, as revenue beat estimates near six billion dollars and its Latin American customer base reached one hundred thirty nine million across Brazil, Mexico, and Colombia.
KinderCare Learning Companies shares collapsed about forty four percent after missing quarterly earnings estimates and cutting full year guidance, citing falling enrollment, lower government childcare subsidy assumptions, and the closure of forty nine centers; JPMorgan downgraded the stock following the report.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

Czech banks issued forty point four billion crowns in new mortgages in July, up seven percent from a year earlier, even as the average rate rose to four point nine percent, pushing monthly payments on new loans up roughly two thousand nine hundred crowns versus last year.
The number of Czechs saving in third pillar pension funds fell by eighteen thousand in the first half of the year, the first such decline in years, as a proposed finance ministry rule would cap fees at zero point five percent and phase out older transformed funds by 2036; assets in these funds have topped seven hundred billion crowns.
Slovakia's economy grew just zero point seven percent year over year in the second quarter, its weakest pace in a year and below forecasts, as investment fell and growth relied only on consumer spending, leaving Slovakia trailing Poland, Czechia, and the broader European Union average, and adding pressure on the government's stalled budget talks.
The Czech National Bank fined CSOB a record thirty million crowns, the largest anti money laundering penalty it has issued, citing inadequate checks on risky cash transactions and incomplete ownership verification on more than eight thousand four hundred corporate clients.

China.

China and Brazil disclosed new details of a joint geostationary satellite program for round the clock disaster early warning across South America, with Brazil building the platform and China supplying the instruments, targeting a 2030 launch that would make Brazil one of the few nations able to build and operate a geostationary satellite.
A levee on the Jialu River in Zhoukou, Henan province, broke under heavy rain from Typhoon Dolphin's remnants, flooding a hospital and a driving school and forcing the evacuation of four hundred thirty two people, including hospital patients and staff, as Beijing dispatched two hundred rescue personnel to the area.
