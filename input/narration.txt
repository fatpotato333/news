Daily News for Friday, August 28, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Anthropic released a research preview of a Model Hardware Standard that lets Claude AI agents directly operate physical lab equipment, including microscopes and liquid handlers, for tasks like drug discovery and quantum hardware calibration, sharing it first with select science and manufacturing partners.

OpenAI led an open letter signed by more than 100 companies, including Anthropic, Google, and Microsoft, along with major cybersecurity and financial firms, calling for a global surge in AI powered cyber defense to protect hospitals, water systems, and internet infrastructure before AI enabled attacks become more sophisticated.

United States stock market.

Salesforce shares jumped about 22 percent, their second best trading day ever, after quarterly revenue and profit far beat estimates and the company sharply raised its full year guidance, while also unveiling an expanded Anthropic partnership called Claudeforce to build AI agents into its business software.

Cybersecurity stocks CrowdStrike and Okta surged 20 and roughly 28 percent respectively after both companies beat earnings estimates and raised guidance, with executives citing rising enterprise security spending driven by the growing use of AI agents.

The Trump administration is considering a second round of semiconductor tariffs that would extend beyond raw chips to finished products like laptops and servers, a report that knocked down chip stocks including Micron as investors weighed the risk to AI data center buildouts.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

Slovakia's government approved a package of laws redirecting child benefit payments to municipalities for families whose children skip school repeatedly, raising school supply assistance from 33 to 55 euros, and restricting child benefits for foreign nationals whose children do not actually live in Slovakia.

The Czech government completed the migration of the mandatory data box digital mail system to its new datovka dot gov dot cz domain, permanently shutting down the old address used by millions of citizens and businesses for official correspondence.

China.

President Trump suggested the United States may already be sanctioning Chinese banks over their dealings with Iran, declining to give details, days after the Treasury sanctioned dozens of entities and ahead of Xi Jinping's expected visit to Washington next month.

China and the United States are discussing whether Chinese business executives will join Xi Jinping on his September trip to Washington, and officials say a one year extension of the countries' trade truce is now considered almost certain.

Chinese Premier Li Qiang visited Tibet's Gyirong region, China's main trade gateway to Nepal, after a glacial flash flood destroyed bridges and roads and left hundreds missing, with hundreds of troops deployed to the disaster zone.
