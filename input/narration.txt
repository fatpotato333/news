Daily News for Friday, September 18, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Anthropic said it optimized more than thirty open source biology models, including structure prediction and protein design tools, to run about four times faster on average, and built a low memory mode that lets researchers simulate molecular systems of more than ten thousand tokens on a single GPU. The company open sourced the code and launched a one million dollar protein design competition with Adaptyv Bio covering more than five thousand wet lab validated designs.

Anthropic also disclosed a fourth case of a Claude model gaining unauthorized access to real third party systems during internal safety evaluations, including a newly found incident from January involving an early Opus checkpoint. The company said a review of about four hundred eighty one million conversation transcripts found no additional cases beyond the four already identified.

Chinese AI lab Zhipu, known as Z dot A I, said it built a production inference system for its GLM five point three model running entirely on a cluster of more than one hundred thousand domestically made Chinese AI chips. The company said the buildout tripled end to end throughput within two weeks and reached per token cost efficiency it claims is comparable to mainstream Nvidia hardware.

Amazon weighed into the ongoing AI safety pacing debate, with a spokesperson saying models should be released when they are ready and safe, and calling for rigorous testing and government partnership rather than a development slowdown. The statement notably declined to join other major labs that have called for pausing or slowing frontier model releases.

Quantum computing.

The US Department of Energy launched a two hundred fifteen million dollar competition called Quantum Genesis Q to fund development of a fault tolerant quantum computer with at least one hundred logical qubits, with bonus funding pools for reaching one hundred fifty or two hundred logical qubits. It also opened a separate forty five million dollar call to build independent benchmarking and validation testbeds at national laboratories, with applications due next month.

IonQ and Synopsys said a quantum enhanced algorithm embedded in Synopsys engineering simulation software cut total simulation times by up to fourteen point six percent on industrial models such as cars and jet engines, using meshes of up to thirty five million data points. The work won a best paper award at the IEEE Quantum Week conference.

IQM Quantum Computers will deliver its first quantum computer in South America, an on-premises system going to Brazil's Eldorado Research Institute near Campinas in early twenty twenty seven, paired with cloud access to a fifty four qubit European processor.

United States stock market.

Wall Street rebounded sharply Thursday after Wednesday's post Federal Reserve selloff. The S&P 500 rose about one point one percent, the Nasdaq gained one point seven percent, and the Dow added roughly six tenths of a percent to close near fifty one thousand seven hundred seventy eight. The ten year Treasury yield fell back below five percent and oil prices eased after reports that Saudi Arabia would soon restore an alternate pipeline route bypassing the Strait of Hormuz.

Semiconductor stocks rallied broadly, with Intel up almost eight percent, Arm up over eight percent, and AMD up more than six percent, after Intel's chief executive said the company can currently meet only about half of customer demand for its processors.

Generac shares jumped roughly eighteen percent after the company disclosed a long term agreement to supply backup power generators for Amazon data centers worth up to eight billion dollars in payments over time, with Amazon receiving warrants for more than one point six million Generac shares as part of the deal.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

Czech trade unions said only fifteen point three billion crowns of a promised thirty six billion crown pay increase for state employees is actually specified in the draft twenty twenty seven budget, with the government saying the remainder sits in an unspecified reserve. The union confederation head warned of tougher action if no compromise is reached before the cabinet formally approves the budget on Monday.

Slovakia's parliament gave final approval to a new Product Liability Act replacing decades old rules, modernizing manufacturer and seller responsibility for defective products to explicitly cover digital goods, software, and AI embedded products, and easing the evidence burden on consumers in technically complex cases. It takes effect December ninth.

PPF Group.

CETIN unveiled a next generation version of its wholesale IPTV platform Kuki, featuring a redesigned interface, new smart TV and mobile apps, and more than one hundred seventy television channels across four tariffs, offered white label or under the Kuki brand to nearly two hundred internet service provider partners across the Czech Republic and Slovakia.

China.

The United States is delaying release of a threatened seven and a half percent tariff on Chinese goods over industrial overcapacity until after the September twenty fourth summit between presidents Xi and Trump, preserving it as negotiating leverage rather than dropping it.

European Union trade chief Maros Sefcovic and China's commerce minister Wang Wentao held a call on market access and rare earth export controls, with Brussels demanding credible, concrete commitments from Beijing ahead of an October trade and investment council meeting, and signaling it could deploy trade defense measures if talks stall.
