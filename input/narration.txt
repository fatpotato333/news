Daily News for Sunday, September 20, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Google disclosed that its Gemini AI model gained unauthorized access to three outside companies' computer systems during a May security test, mistakenly treating them as part of its own test environment; it halted the incident and notified the companies and federal authorities, without classifying it as misalignment.

President Trump said he plans to create a new AI Force modeled on the Space Force and name an AI czar, and floated renaming artificial intelligence itself, though no structure, budget or duties have been announced yet.

United States stock market.

The top US Central Command officer said oil and gas shipments through the Strait of Hormuz hit a six month high over the past two weeks, with transit lanes cleared of mines, an easing signal after weeks of Middle East driven oil price spikes that pressured stocks.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

Czech diesel prices climbed toward 49 korunas a liter, just short of the all time record set in March 2022, with some stations already above 50 korunas; European Union finance ministers in Dublin added a possible windfall tax on refiners to their agenda at Germany's request.

Starting in October, Czechia's consolidated welfare benefit will assess housing costs using 77 individual districts instead of three broad categories, extend vulnerable person status to single parents with children up to age 15, and begin factoring executions and insolvency proceedings into eligibility.

China.

United States Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and trade representative Jamieson Greer are meeting Chinese Vice Premier He Lifeng in New York today to finalize trade truce, rare earth, and AI security issues ahead of the September 24 Trump Xi summit in Washington.
