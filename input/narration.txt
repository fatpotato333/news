Daily News for Wednesday, August 12, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Google's Gemini app passed one billion monthly active users, calling it the company's fastest growing product ever, with heavy voice use, screen sharing in Gemini Live, and more than one hundred fifty million images generated every day.

Nvidia released Nemotron three point five Lightning, a thirty billion parameter open weight model built for high volume agentic tasks that runs on a single consumer graphics card, alongside an open source tool called NeMo Switchyard for routing between AI agents.

Quantum computing.

Quantinuum, trading publicly for the first time, reported quarterly revenue up two hundred seventy nine percent to eight million dollars and said its Helios trapped ion system reached near five nines logical fidelity using a new error correction code; it also announced partnerships with Oracle and HPE to bring quantum computing into cloud and high performance computing systems.

United States stock market.

July inflation data showed consumer prices rose just zero point one percent for the month and three point four percent from a year earlier, both cooler than expected, lifting futures on hopes the Federal Reserve has room to cut rates in September.

Cloud computing firm CoreWeave beat second quarter estimates with revenue up one hundred twelve percent to two point six billion dollars and raised its full year guidance, sending shares up about twenty percent on continued strong demand for AI computing capacity.

Super Micro Computer reported quarterly profit far above expectations, with earnings per share of one dollar seventy cents against a forecast of ninety two cents, and disclosed a record order backlog above sixty billion dollars driven by AI server demand.

Shares of running shoe maker On Holding plunged about twenty two percent, their worst day on record, after the company missed second quarter sales estimates and cut its full year growth guidance, citing a slowdown in the promotional US running market.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

Slovakia's government unveiled an eight point drought relief package for farmers as extreme drought now covers about fifty nine percent of the country, moving forward roughly two hundred million euros in EU and national payment advances to mid September and easing grazing and organic farming rules for affected producers.

Czech digital government officials are asking forty thousand volunteers to log into the eDoklady digital ID app simultaneously on Thursday to stress test it ahead of autumn municipal elections, after last year's parliamentary vote crashed the system; officials say updating the app and confirming your PIN in advance should prevent problems.

PPF Group.

PPF Group is building a new investment company to offer investment products to clients of Air Bank and PPF banka, hiring Michal Semotan from J&T and Patrik Hudec from Generali to lead the new unit pending regulatory approval.

China.
