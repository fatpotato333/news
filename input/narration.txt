Daily News for Wednesday, November 4, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Oracle scrapped a roughly seven billion dollar, five year lease that would have given Tencent access to about one hundred thousand advanced AI chips through Oracle data centers in Southeast Asia, after US officials raised concerns about the deal; neither company has confirmed the report.
Nvidia reportedly told cloud and data center partners worldwide to keep at least fifty one percent of new compute services revenue from customers outside China, according to the Financial Times; the directive applies to new contracts, and Nvidia has not confirmed or denied it.
Meta priced a fifteen billion dollar, five part bond sale across maturities running to twenty fifty six to help fund its AI infrastructure buildout, with investor demand reaching about fifty eight billion dollars, roughly four times the amount offered.
Apple opened a developer preview letting any third party app integrate with its new conversational Siri AI through multi step actions, with App Store submissions opening in spring and public rollout expected a few months after that.

Quantum computing.

Physicists at the University of Cologne and RWTH Aachen University observed highly transmissive Andreev states in topological insulator Josephson junctions that match theoretical predictions, a peer reviewed result published in Science Advances marking a step toward topological superconductivity, though a true topological phase transition remains unconfirmed.
EigenOrbit open sourced eigenkit, a provider neutral tool that benchmarks quantum circuits across twelve simulator and hardware backends including IBM and IQM processors, finding no single backend performs best for every workload and that the right choice depends on circuit size and structure.

United States stock market.

US stocks closed mixed on Election Day as chip stocks rebounded broadly from their recent bear market; the S&P 500 rose about four tenths of a percent to a record and the Nasdaq rose about eight tenths of a percent, while the Dow fell nearly two hundred points on weakness tied to Apple's earnings.
AMD reported third quarter revenue of thirteen point four billion dollars, up forty five percent from a year earlier, with data center revenue up ninety seven percent on strong EPYC and new MI450 chip sales; shares jumped more than seven percent after hours as AMD guided fourth quarter revenue to about seventeen billion dollars.
Palantir reported third quarter revenue of two point three billion dollars, up ninety six percent year over year and its first quarter above two billion, with US commercial revenue exceeding government revenue for the first time and full year guidance raised, yet shares fell nearly two percent on lingering valuation concerns.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

European gas prices jumped above ninety eight euros per megawatt hour after reports of a US strike near Iran's Qeshm Island in the Strait of Hormuz, with European Union gas storage already below seasonal norms at under seventy five percent full, raising the risk of higher winter heating bills in both countries.
Slovakia's approved twenty twenty seven budget sets aside only two hundred fifty million euros for household energy aid, below the finance ministry's own estimate of three hundred forty to four hundred thirty million euros needed, with tightened eligibility rules expected to cut coverage from about ninety percent of households to about eighty six percent.
Slovakia's association of towns and municipalities, ZMOS, warned that household waste collection fees could rise by seven to twenty euros per person starting January twenty twenty seven, due to a new mandatory waste treatment requirement plus higher minimum wage and fuel costs feeding into local budgets.

PPF Group.

PPF Group sold a roughly seven thousand square meter office building in central The Hague, partly occupied by a Dutch state government office, for about one hundred sixty million Czech koruna, as part of ongoing real estate portfolio optimization.
PPF Banka provided long term financing worth a combined roughly forty two million euros for battery storage projects in Germany and Romania on behalf of the RSJ Energy fund, its third such deal for that fund.

China.
