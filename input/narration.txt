Daily News for Monday, October 5, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

President Trump named Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton to chair a newly created Super Intelligence Force, ordering a four person panel to deliver a report on AI risks and opportunities within 120 days.

United States stock market.

Yemen's Houthi movement claimed missile and drone strikes on Saudi Aramco facilities in Riyadh and Khurais, briefly spiking oil prices before Brent and West Texas Intermediate both eased on Monday as rising Middle East exports and a planned G7 reserve release calmed supply fears.

Seagate and Western Digital shares fell sharply in Monday premarket trading after a report that Toshiba plans to roughly double its hard disk drive production capacity for AI data centers by 2027, threatening the tight supply that had driven both stocks up sharply this year.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

Slovenska sporitelna, Slovakia's largest bank, raised mortgage rates across all fixation periods effective today, the fourth Slovak bank to hike in about a month, pushing most rates further above four percent.

China.

The Philippines accused China of harassing its forces twice within 24 hours in the South China Sea. A Chinese fighter jet buzzed a Philippine surveillance plane near Scarborough Shoal, while Chinese coast guard ships and a navy helicopter blocked a Philippine patrol targeting illegal fishing near Thitu Island.

A Chinese survey ship escorted by coast guard vessels spoofed its tracking signal while surveying the seabed near undersea cable routes in the Bashi Channel between Taiwan and the Philippines, prompting both countries to shadow the vessel.

China's foreign ministry rejected an American lawmaker's demand that Starbucks close its newly opened stores in Xinjiang over forced labor concerns, calling the underlying allegations a lie.

Chinese researchers unveiled a pilot plant that extracts hydrogen, fresh water, uranium, and bromine from seawater in one integrated system, a potential efficiency gain for future energy and mineral supply.
