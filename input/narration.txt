Daily News for Tuesday, November 24, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

OpenAI and Cisco released Shirudo, an open source tool that uses signed, verified software shields to block attacks on a known vulnerability in live production systems without needing a restart, while a permanent patch is tested.

United States stock market.

Nvidia's board reinstated Jensen Huang as president and chief executive, less than two weeks after he stepped back amid an investigation into an alleged relationship with a Chinese businesswoman that U.S. officials suspected had ties to Chinese intelligence. The board said it found no national security risk, but created a new national security and export control committee, and said an independent investigation into the allegations will continue. Multiple outlets reported the reinstatement came after President Trump pressed for Huang's return, saying the recent market slump had influenced his thinking. Nvidia shares jumped about thirty one percent, adding roughly nine hundred fifty billion dollars in value, the largest one day gain for any stock in history, and lifted the broader market out of last week's correction, with the S&P 500 up almost five percent and the Nasdaq up more than six percent.

Micron Technology shares rose nineteen percent after the company secured more multi year memory supply deals with customers, as tight supply of D-RAM and high bandwidth memory chips continues to support pricing.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

The Czech government extended its emergency price caps on diesel and gasoline, plus a cut to the diesel excise tax, through the end of twenty twenty six rather than letting the measure expire this month. The caps limit retailer margins and are currently holding diesel around forty four crowns per liter and gasoline around thirty nine crowns, though the extension will cost the state budget roughly one billion crowns a month in lost tax revenue.

A survey of more than eight hundred Czech employers found companies plan to raise base wages by an average of four point three percent in twenty twenty seven in the private sector, compared with six point five percent in the public sector, while the Czech National Bank forecasts twenty twenty seven inflation at three point two percent.

In Slovakia, the government again declined to exempt municipalities from the constitutional debt brake for twenty twenty seven, and the opposition party Progressive Slovakia warned many towns may have to adopt stopgap provisional budgets that could delay basic local services.

China.

A Chinese ministry official was sentenced to death for leaking more than one hundred state secrets, with China's secrecy watchdog saying foreign operatives used money and blackmail to recruit the official as a double agent, part of Beijing's intensifying counter espionage campaign.

China's anti corruption watchdog opened an investigation into Liu Min, a senior science ministry official who previously directed major national technology projects.

Mexico and China agreed to form a bilateral working group to boost trade, with Mexico pressing for easier access to the Chinese market for its farm and food exports.
