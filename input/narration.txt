Daily News for Sunday, October 18, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

DeepSeek released an updated version of its V4.1 Flash model, which the company says is faster and performs better on benchmarks than its predecessor. The improvements are based on company claims and have not been independently verified.

United States stock market.

AI cloud provider CoreWeave's bonds and stock fell again Friday, with yields on its 2031 and 2032 notes spiking above fifteen percent and the cost of insuring its debt near one thousand basis points, reflecting growing worry about its fifty billion dollar debt load.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

The Czech Ministry of Industry revised the rules for its planned capacity auction supporting new gas fired backup power plants, pushing the launch from late this year into 2027. The program is meant to distribute up to roughly one hundred fifty billion crowns to replace retiring coal capacity.

China.

People's Bank of China governor Pan Gongsheng told the International Monetary Fund's steering committee in Bangkok that China supports closer IMF monitoring of fiscal risks in major advanced economies, and renewed Beijing's call for stronger backing of the multilateral trading system.
