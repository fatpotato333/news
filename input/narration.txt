Daily News for Tuesday, August 4, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Microsoft's AI division appears to be testing an unreleased voice model called MAI Realtime, a full duplex system supporting real time conversation in about seventeen languages, spotted in an internal preview; Microsoft has not confirmed or launched it.

Quantum computing.

Caltech and the startup Oratomic published a new quantum error correction code called a mitten code that reached roughly twenty percent encoding efficiency in simulation, outperforming a much larger surface code design by nearly two orders of magnitude; the work is an unreviewed preprint, not yet tested on hardware.

United States stock market.

Amazon's market value topped three trillion dollars for the first time on Monday, making it the fifth company ever to reach that level, extending a rally from its strong cloud earnings.

Palantir Technologies reported second quarter revenue of about one point nine billion dollars, up ninety three percent from a year earlier, and raised its full year guidance to roughly eight point one five billion dollars; shares jumped nearly fifteen percent in after hours trading.

The Institute for Supply Management's manufacturing index for July rose to fifty five point six, the highest reading since May 2022, marking a seventh straight month of expansion.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

In Slovakia, labor inspectors can now audit any employer for equal pay between men and women starting this week; unexplained wage gaps over five percent must be corrected, with fines of up to one hundred thousand euros for violations.

PPF Group.

The e and PPF Telecom joint venture, which includes CETIN's international infrastructure business across Slovakia, Hungary, Bulgaria and Serbia, reported first half revenue up fourteen percent to one point two nine two billion euros and net profit up fifteen percent to two hundred twenty three million euros.

China.
