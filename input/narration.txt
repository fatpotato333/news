Quantum computing.

Independent researchers in Spain ran a standardized entanglement benchmark across nine cloud-accessible quantum processors from five vendors, including IBM, IQM, Rigetti, and AQT. The best performer, a 120 qubit IBM chip, sustained genuine multi-qubit entanglement in only about a quarter of its qubits, and just four of the nine systems cleared a stricter entanglement threshold at all, showing that advertised qubit counts do not reliably predict real world performance.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

Hundreds of thousands of people protested on Prague's Letna plain on Sunday against a government bill to abolish mandatory license fees for Czech Television and Czech Radio. Prime Minister Andrej Babis said Monday he respects the right to protest but confirmed the fees will be scrapped regardless, with the state proposing to fund the broadcasters directly starting in January twenty twenty seven.

The Czech government is set to raise compensation for workplace injuries, occupational diseases, and survivor benefits by point two percent plus a flat two hundred seventy crowns, mirroring January's pension increase, effective from January twenty twenty seven for employees as well as police officers and soldiers.
