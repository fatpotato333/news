United States stock market.

Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh struck a hawkish tone in his first Jackson Hole speech, saying inflation trends have not meaningfully improved. Traders raised the odds of a September rate hike, Treasury yields rose, and stocks pulled back Friday.

Marvell Technology shares fell sharply even after beating earnings estimates and raising revenue guidance, as investors focused on weaker margin guidance and a lack of detail on its Google AI chip partnership.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

The Czech Labor Ministry proposed cutting unemployment benefits starting January twenty twenty seven to save four and a half billion crowns, lowering the initial payout rate from eighty percent to sixty five percent of prior wages and reducing the maximum monthly cap.

Slovalco restarted primary aluminum production in Ziar nad Hronom after a three year shutdown, backed by more than one hundred million euros in investment and a government energy cost support package expected to create thousands of jobs at full capacity.

China.
