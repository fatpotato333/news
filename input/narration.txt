Daily News for Saturday, October 24, 2026.

Quantum computing.

Quantum computing company IQM and telecom operator Telefónica will install the Madrid region's first quantum computer, an IQM Spark system, at the Technical University of Madrid before the end of this year, giving researchers, students, and startups direct access to the hardware. Google Quantum AI launched a one hundred fifty thousand dollar competition on Kaggle challenging teams to shrink the number of qubits and logic gates needed to run quantum algorithms for chemistry, optimization, and other tasks, aiming to cut the hardware burden that quantum error correction imposes.

United States stock market.

US stocks fell Friday, with the Dow down one point two percent and the S&P 500 off about half a percent, as investors grew uneasy about the pace of artificial intelligence data center spending while oil prices jumped nearly four percent on rising Middle East tensions. Lockheed Martin beat third quarter expectations, posting revenue of twenty point seven billion dollars, up eleven percent from a year earlier, and raised its full year profit guidance. Kering, the parent company of Gucci, saw its shares drop about twelve percent after reporting weaker than expected third quarter results.

China.

China's National Development and Reform Commission finalized new outbound investment rules taking effect December first, requiring Chinese companies to report more quickly when foreign governments pressure them to hand over technology, data, or force the sale of overseas assets.
