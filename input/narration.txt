Daily News for Sunday, October 25, 2026.

United States stock market.

American Express posted record third quarter revenue of twenty point one billion dollars, up nine percent, and raised its full year profit guidance, though the stock lagged behind in Friday's broader market pullback.

Oilfield services company SLB beat estimates for the third quarter, reporting nine point four five billion dollars in revenue as energy stocks benefited from rising oil prices.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

Czech lawmakers advanced a bill in an overnight vote early Saturday to scrap the television and radio license fee and fund public broadcasting directly from the state budget instead, over opposition objections that it could weaken media independence.

The Czech parliament locked in a record three hundred eighty six billion koruna budget deficit for next year after a marathon overnight debate, with the opposition's bid to shrink it voted down.

Trade unions at Skoda Auto pushed back against Volkswagen's push to freeze pay through 2030 under its cost cutting plan, insisting the existing labor agreement, not group headquarters in Germany, governs their wages.

China.

Typhoon Champi, the world's strongest storm so far this year, skirted China's east coast, grounding more than a hundred flights and forcing tens of thousands of evacuations in Zhejiang and Fujian provinces.

The Pentagon added Beijing backed Confucius Institutes on American university campuses to its list of national security threats, escalating Washington's broader crackdown on Chinese academic ties that began earlier this year.
