Daily News for Sunday, October 4, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Meta said its Muse Spark AI model helped mathematicians crack five long unsolved problems, but a reviewer checking the work says roughly half of those problems had already been solved independently months earlier.

Tesla cut memory capacity on its upcoming AI5 and AI6 chips by as much as half, freeing up scarce memory supply to prioritize mass production of its Optimus humanoid robot, according to Elon Musk.

Quantum computing.

India's IT ministry said researchers at QNu Labs, BISAG-N, and IIT Gandhinagar successfully tested a free space quantum key distribution link spanning five and a half kilometers, generating secure encryption keys that were then combined with post quantum cryptography software.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

The Czech Energy Regulatory Office's indicative energy prices jumped fourteen percent for electricity and twenty percent for gas in October compared with September, mainly affecting households whose fixed price contracts are expiring or who are signing new energy deals.

The Czech Finance Ministry ordered internet providers to block the American prediction market platform Kalshi, adding it to the list of unauthorized gambling services after already blocking Polymarket earlier this year.

A new survey found fifty six percent of Czech workers now use AI on the job, up from a third two years ago, though nearly half worry AI could replace their work, with the sharpest anxiety among those aged eighteen to thirty.

China.

Huawei said its homegrown Kirin Tau chip, built to work around US restrictions on advanced chipmaking tools, is on track to power more than one million Mate XT2 foldable phone sales.
