Daily News for Friday, October 2, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Anthropic said Barclays is expanding its use of Claude across its global operations, aiming for half of its developer population to use Claude Code by the end of this year and a majority of engineers by twenty twenty seven, building on an internal assistant already used by more than sixteen thousand UK staff for over one million searches and models that now process some one hundred twenty thousand client emails a day in the bank's global markets unit.

OpenAI confirmed it dismissed three safety researchers after an internal investigation found they shared confidential company information with an outside AI safety organization outside approved channels, according to a Wall Street Journal report that OpenAI did not dispute.

Quantum computing.

D-Wave opened a beta program for a twenty one qubit gate model quantum simulator built on its superconducting cavity qubit architecture with built in error detection, with early access partners including the bank BBVA, Florida Atlantic University, and Germany's Jülich Supercomputing Centre, as part of the company's push toward fault tolerant hardware.

Researchers at the University of Surrey and Northwestern University published a peer reviewed design for a new type of qubit using superfluid helium to shield it from electromagnetic noise, predicting error rates roughly one hundred times lower than today's superconducting qubits, though the design remains theoretical and no working device has been built yet.

United States stock market.

The ten year Treasury yield climbed to about five point three percent, its highest in roughly twenty four years, before easing slightly as stocks closed mixed, with the S&P five hundred edging up two tenths of a percent and the Nasdaq and Dow both little changed, as investors weighed inflation concerns tied to rising energy prices, strong growth data, and surging government debt.

Accenture shares surged as much as twenty two percent, their best single trading day on record, after the company beat fourth quarter earnings and revenue estimates and issued a stronger than expected revenue outlook for next year, a sharp reversal after the stock had fallen roughly forty percent so far this year.

Nike shares fell about four percent to their lowest level in more than a decade despite beating profit estimates, as the company guided for a high single digit percentage revenue decline next year amid continued sales weakness in China and Europe.

Nvidia's plan to help arrange roughly five hundred billion dollars in financing for artificial intelligence data center buildouts, in talks with firms including Blackstone, Goldman Sachs, and KKR, is facing skepticism from banks and asset managers over how long its chips will retain enough value to serve as loan collateral.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

The Czech government reimposed daily price caps on petrol and diesel and cut the diesel excise tax starting October first, a month long measure meant to ease pump prices that is expected to cost the state budget roughly one point one billion crowns in lost tax revenue, partly offset by a new special tax on refinery margins.

Slovakia capped retail fuel margins at ten euro cents per liter and cut second class train fares in half starting October first, with discounts of thirty percent on regional buses and fifty percent on city transit also planned for the month to ease living costs.

A reform of the Czech unified benefit system took effect October first, raising the living minimum for single adults from four thousand eight hundred sixty to five thousand five hundred crowns a month and extending extra housing and income protections for single parents to those with children up to age fifteen, benefiting an estimated twenty eight thousand households with an average gain of over two thousand crowns a month.

China.

Group of twenty trade ministers meeting in Milwaukee failed to reach agreement on steel and industrial overcapacity, with the United States defending a new framework grouping twenty eight countries against overcapacity while pointedly excluding China, the world's largest steel producer, days after a state visit by Chinese leader Xi Jinping.

The Philippine coast guard sent its largest ship and an aircraft to challenge a Chinese survey vessel operating near Taiwan, prompting a Chinese coast guard vessel to intervene and Beijing's embassy in Manila to accuse the Philippines of harassing what it called routine scientific research.

China's finance ministry, central bank, and financial regulator began directly subsidizing mortgage interest for first time buyers of modest homes starting October first, offering one percentage point off loan rates for up to five years, the central government's first ever direct subsidy of commercial home loan interest, aimed at supporting a weak property market and consumer spending.
