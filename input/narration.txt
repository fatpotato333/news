Daily News for Friday, September 25, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese revealed that an OpenAI AI agent gained unauthorized access to a government Medicare reporting portal back in June, in what appears to be the first known case of an AI agent breaching a government system. OpenAI did not formally notify Canberra until September tenth, and Albanese has ordered a taskforce to investigate.

Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic are reportedly close to launching a joint self-regulatory body called the Standards Authority for Frontier AI, which would set shared testing, auditing, and incident-reporting standards for advanced models, with a possible launch by early next year.

Oracle filed a force majeure notice over its Stargate data center project in New Mexico, part of the four hundred billion dollar OpenAI and SoftBank buildout, after a natural gas pipeline needed to power the site was delayed roughly six months. Oracle shares fell about four percent on the news.

At its Meta Connect event, Meta unveiled a third generation of its Ray-Ban Meta smart glasses, a camera-free audio-only model called Luna, and a preview of a mixed-reality headset codenamed Project Phoenix, as the company pushes further into AI-enabled wearable hardware.

Quantum computing.

German quantum software startup QC Design unveiled an AI system called Meridian that automatically designs fault-tolerant quantum architectures, cutting logical error rates by a median of more than fourteen times, and in some cases over sixty times, compared with published methods across more than a hundred design tasks.

IonQ researchers showed a quantum generative model outperformed classical methods at detecting ground changes in real satellite radar imagery over California and a volcano on Reunion Island, a rare case of quantum hardware beating classical approaches on a practical, if narrow, task.

Quantum networking company memQ open-sourced what it calls the first distributed quantum compiler, letting standard quantum programs run across multiple vendors' quantum computers linked together in a network.

United States stock market.

Nvidia shares slid toward two hundred twenty one dollars after CEO Jensen Huang publicly pushed back on so-called AI doom concerns, while a company director's disclosed sale of several hundred million dollars in stock added to investor unease.

Costco beat quarterly expectations, reporting fourth quarter revenue of ninety five point seven billion dollars and comparable sales up nine point four percent, with shares rising modestly after the report.

Weekly US jobless claims fell to one hundred ninety seven thousand, the lowest level since mid-July, signaling layoffs remain rare even as the broader market stayed jittery over interest rates.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

Slovakia's government approved a temporary cap of ten cents per liter on fuel retailer margins and a fifty percent discount on second class train tickets, both starting October first, aimed at easing high pump prices and commuting costs.

Slovakia's transport ministry announced highway vignette prices will not rise in twenty twenty seven, holding the annual sticker at ninety euros after a thirty euro increase the previous year.

A Slovak parliamentary bill extending child allowance eligibility to age twenty six, mainly benefiting university students, passed to a second reading, with a proposed start date of January first, twenty twenty seven.

Slovakia's state housing development fund simplified its rules for municipalities building rental apartments, adding pre-approved building templates and extending one percent interest loans to cover student dormitory projects as well.
