Daily News for Thursday, September 24, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

Anthropic said its new Claude-powered biology research lab autonomously discovered a novel enzyme system inside bacteriophage DNA, deploying nearly a thousand AI agent instances over twenty one hours to scan two hundred thousand proteins. The enzyme system's function is still unknown, and the finding has not yet been peer reviewed.

OpenAI's Sam Altman and Anthropic's Dario Amodei briefed the United Nations Security Council on AI risk, in a session that also included China's DeepSeek and Moonshot AI. Amodei proposed a global ban on recursively self improving AI plus mutual inspection standards, while Altman called for international measures of AI capability and risk.

Google launched Gemini three point eight Flash text to speech, offering more than two thousand production voices, thirty second voice cloning with digital watermarking, and support for over a hundred languages; the model topped the Hume Voice Design benchmark.

Alibaba's Qwen team released a new audio model lineup, Qwen Audio three point one, and cut its audio API prices by as much as ninety five percent, with its speech recognition model now supporting thirty languages and sixteen Chinese dialects.

Nvidia released Nemotron three Diarization, an open weight speaker identification model with about one hundred million parameters that can distinguish up to eight overlapping speakers, runs on just four gigabytes of GPU memory, and cuts errors by roughly forty one percent versus its predecessor.

Quantum computing.

Microsoft opened a new fifteen thousand square foot Quantum Research Center in Maryland and gave the Pentagon's research agency, DARPA, on site access to independently test its second generation Majorana topological qubit chip, addressing past scientific skepticism about the technology's claims.

United States stock market.

US stocks fell sharply as the ten year Treasury yield jumped to its highest level since two thousand seven, after stronger than expected September business activity data and hawkish comments from Federal Reserve officials raised bets on another rate hike in October; the S and P 500 dropped about eight tenths of one percent and the Nasdaq fell about one percent.

Shares of Expedia, Airbnb and Booking Holdings tumbled between five and seven percent on fears that Meta's new AI shopping agent, Muse, could let users book travel directly and bypass their platforms.

Bamboo Insurance postponed its planned New York Stock Exchange listing, which had aimed to raise up to seven hundred million dollars, citing volatile market conditions.

McDonald's shares fell as much as six percent after the company pushed back its long-standing goal of reaching fifty thousand restaurants worldwide from twenty twenty seven to twenty twenty eight, citing pressure on consumer spending and rising construction costs, alongside soft same-store sales growth in the US.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

Slovakia's parliament definitively passed a law granting delivery and ride hailing platform workers, such as couriers for Bolt and Wolt, a legal presumption of employee status, a ban on off hours monitoring, and a right to human review of account suspensions, taking effect in December.

Slovak lawmakers also approved a reform of social services financing tied to long term elderly and disability care, changing funding rules and provider support.

China.

Xi Jinping arrived in Washington for a summit with President Trump covering Taiwan, trade and artificial intelligence, and Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced the two countries' trade truce has been extended to January tenth, twenty twenty seven, from its earlier November deadline.
