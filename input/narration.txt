Artificial intelligence.

DeepSeek made its V4 Pro model generally available across its app, web, and API, adding agentic tool use, a one million token context window, and compatibility with OpenAI's Codex interface, alongside a new peak and off peak pricing structure that raises rates for both V4 Pro and V4 Flash.
OpenAI introduced Ultrafast, a new API tier that runs its GPT 5.6 Sol model up to fourteen times faster, at up to seven hundred fifty output tokens per second, using Cerebras hardware, with no claimed loss in intelligence, rolling out first to select API customers.
Larsen and Toubro will build one of India's largest single cluster AI computing installations, deploying about ten thousand Nvidia B300 chips at its Chennai data center campus for the American company Together AI, in a deal worth roughly one to two billion dollars.
Microsoft cut its purchases of carbon removal credits by about eighty percent over the past year, to around eight and a half million metric tons, as it redirects capital toward AI data center buildout.

Quantum computing.

The Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency awarded quantum networking company Qunnect a contract to build a new real time signal stabilization module for its entanglement distribution hardware, which underpins operational metro quantum networks already running in New York, Bozeman, Berlin, and Albuquerque.

United States stock market.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

Czech fuel prices have stopped falling now that last month's temporary price cap has ended, with gasoline averaging about forty one crowns and diesel about forty four crowns per liter, both still well above year ago levels, and analysts expecting prices to hold steady for now barring new Middle East tensions.
Slovak inflation slowed to three point three percent in July, its lowest in nineteen months, as food prices fell, but housing and energy costs jumped seven point four percent, the steepest rise in three years, with heating bills up roughly twenty eight percent since January.

China.

A White House report accused Chinese exporters of routing about seventy five billion dollars a year in goods through more than forty countries, including several Latin American nations, to disguise their origin and dodge US tariffs, an evasion the report estimates costs nineteen to twenty six billion dollars a year in lost US tariff revenue.
China's top two chipmakers, SMIC and Hua Hong, posted sharply higher second quarter profits, with SMIC's net profit up over two hundred sixty percent and revenue above three billion dollars for the first time, as domestic AI chip demand and tight capacity pushed prices up despite US export controls.
A levee on the Beiru River in Henan province broke after torrential rain of more than four hundred millimeters in two days, forcing the evacuation of more than six thousand five hundred people across two cities, though no casualties were reported.
China conducted a joint naval exercise with an Indonesian warship in waters east of Taiwan, which the US State Department called destabilizing and urged Beijing to halt, and which Taiwan's foreign ministry called a malicious provocation, while Indonesia described it as a routine passing exercise.
