Daily News for Tuesday, September 8, 2026.

Quantum computing.

Sparrow Quantum, working with Ruhr University Bochum, unveiled a deterministic photon source delivering more than 500 million usable photons per second into optical fiber, the highest single photon flux reported to date. The device operates at 1 gigahertz with over 50 percent fiber coupling efficiency, enabling multi photon interference experiments previously out of reach for quantum computing and quantum networking research.

China.

China's customs agency reported August exports rose 25 percent from a year earlier, driven by strong demand for autos, semiconductors, and AI infrastructure hardware, widening the trade surplus to 119.1 billion dollars. Separately, authorities in Wuhan opened an investigation after drone footage showed barges dumping an unidentified black substance into the Yangtze River near an islet that supplies the city's drinking water.

Czech Republic and Slovakia.

The Czech government approved a 2027 minimum wage of 24,900 crowns per month, an increase of 11.2 percent, taking effect January 1st. Related minimum insurance payments for self-employed workers and people without taxable income will also rise. The government separately decided against capping fuel retailer margins, with Prime Minister Andrej Babiš saying rising pump prices stem from refinery capacity shortages and Middle East supply disruption rather than retailer markups.
