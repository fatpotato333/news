Artificial intelligence.

Anthropic and OpenAI are pursuing smaller, twenty to thirty megawatt data center deals in the United Kingdom, the Nordics and the United States, shifting away from giant training campuses toward faster to deploy inference capacity.

Anthropic and Accenture announced a partnership investing more than one billion dollars over five years to embed Accenture evaluators inside Anthropic, with employee level access to red team Claude models and test safety safeguards.

Anthropic disclosed that Claude now leads roughly twenty six percent of the work that builds Claude itself, up from under one percent in February, with about thirty thousand agents now running concurrently inside the company.

OpenAI launched Astra for Law, pairing its GPT-6 Astra model with a two hundred thirty million page legal index; on internal benchmarks it scored fifty four percent correctness, versus thirty eight point seven percent for the same model using web search alone.

Quantum computing.

Chattanooga utility EPB activated an IonQ Forte Enterprise trapped ion quantum computer at a new Quantum Center, planning to apply it to power grid optimization, with full commercial operation opening in early October.

United States stock market.

US stocks closed a volatile week mixed on Friday: the S and P 500 rose slightly and the Nasdaq gained about four tenths of a percent, but the Dow slipped, with both the Dow and S and P still down for the week following the Federal Reserve's first rate hike in three years.

Nucor fell about five percent and Steel Dynamics fell three to four and a half percent after both steelmakers guided third quarter earnings below Wall Street estimates; Steel Dynamics cited Canada's new fifty percent retaliatory tariffs on U.S. steel and aluminum.

Homebuilder Lennar missed third quarter earnings estimates and cut its full year delivery guidance to between eighty thousand and eighty one thousand homes, citing higher mortgage rates following the Fed's rate hike and stiffer competition from resale homes.

China.
