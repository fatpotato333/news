Daily News for Saturday, August 22, 2026.

Artificial intelligence.

DeepSeek released an experimental multimodal model called V4 Flash Vision Exp, adding vision and agentic capabilities and claiming it nearly matches Anthropic's Claude Opus four point eight on some benchmarks, though it trails badly on the hardest test. The claims come from DeepSeek's own benchmarks and are not independently verified.

Quantum computing.

Brookhaven National Laboratory and Stony Brook University demonstrated the first free space quantum network link in the United States, sending entangled photons through open air across about thirteen miles on Long Island, linking into an existing fiber based quantum network with a third station planned at Yale.

United States stock market.

Major U.S. stock indexes rebounded Friday, with the Dow gaining about five hundred eighteen points, but still closed the week lower, as long term Treasury yields stayed near their highest level since 2007 despite the Treasury's newly expanded bond buyback program.

China.

A court in Shenzhen sentenced Evergrande founder Xu Jiayin to life in prison for fraud and illegal fundraising, and fined Evergrande entities a combined fifteen point eight billion yuan, about two point two billion dollars, with fifty six co-defendants also sentenced.

China's foreign ministry rejected a public request from U.S. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent for Beijing to join Washington's sanctions campaign against Iran, saying pressure tactics are not the solution, and signaled it will keep facilitating Iranian oil purchases.

Shanghai became the first major Chinese city to ease home buying rules since July's weak economic data, cutting the minimum down payment on second homes to fifteen percent from twenty percent and adding resale subsidies of up to eighty thousand yuan.
